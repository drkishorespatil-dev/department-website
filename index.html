Welcome Note

About the Department

Empowering healthcare education and drug monitoring at the core of Maharashtra.

The Department of Pharmacology is primarily responsible for teaching pharmacology to MBBS, BDS, MD Pharmacology, CCMP, and B.Sc Nursing students. Additionally, the department shoulders the critical responsibility of managing the Medical Drug Store of the Hospital, ensuring uninterrupted supply to OPD and IPD patients.

Vision & Goals

  • Developing our PvPi Center with an emphasis on creating community awareness on Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) reporting.
  • Development of an advanced Drug Information Center in the near future.
  • Starting clinical pharmacology consultation lines for personalized drug therapy.

Key Areas

  • Pharmacokinetics & Pharmacodynamics: Mechanics of drugs inside the human body.
  • Toxicology: Detection and management of poisonings and drug toxicities.
  • Rational Drug Use: Campaigning for the prescription of essential medicines.
Dean's Initiative

QR-Code Based Student Information Initiative

With the inspiration of the Hon'ble Dean, Dr. Shivaji Sukre, Government Medical College, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, and under the guidance of the Head of the Department of Pharmacology, Dr. Mirza Shiraz Baig, the Department of Pharmacology has launched "Department Information for Students" — a QR-code based initiative.

Through this initiative, students of MBBS, BDS, B.Sc Nursing and CCMP will receive guidance about the department's academic programs and teaching schedules, rational use of medicines, adverse drug reaction (ADR) reporting, and the services available in the department.
